How the cuts are decided

    CPAK classification

    aHKA = MPTA − LDFA   (negative is varus)
    JLO = MPTA + LDFA   (below 177° apex distal, above 183° apex proximal)

    Varus is aHKA below −2°, neutral −2° to +2°, valgus above +2°. Three alignment groups × three joint line groups give nine phenotypes. Mechanical alignment turns every knee into Type V, which is why personalised strategies exist.

    Schematic teaching model, right knee viewed from the front with lateral on the left. Boundary values (rKA: components ±5°, HKA ±3°; iKA: tibia ±5°, HKA ±3° corrected through the femur; FA: components ±3°) are illustrative and vary between authors and robotic systems. Real functional alignment also adjusts sagittal and rotational position using measured gaps, which this 2-D model cannot show.
